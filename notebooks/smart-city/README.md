# Smart city pages (lesson 11)

Single-file browser pages for [lesson 11](../../lessons/11-government-smart-city/README.md). Each one opens directly
in a browser, loads nothing from the network, and sends nothing anywhere.

| File | Case | Data | Published |
|---|---|---|---|
| `flood-watch.html` | Case 1 · Flood Watch: a 2-hour bayou forecast, an alert line, gauges going dark | USGS water-level gauges on Brays Bayou (public domain), Harvey 2017 and Beryl 2024 replays | Yes |
| `storm-desk.html` | Case 2 · Storm Desk: sign off on AI-drafted flood alerts in four languages | Recorded model drafts built only from the Case 1 readings and flood stages | Yes |
| `synthetic-residents.html` | Can the public trust it? 300 AI "residents" answer the government-spending questions that 300 real people already answered | General Social Survey 2024 (NORC at the University of Chicago, free with citation; cited on the page) and recorded model answers | Yes |

The pages are built in the course module folder and copied here unchanged. The Studio's `/city-operations` console
replays the same storms with its own recorded data.
