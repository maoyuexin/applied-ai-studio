Model, data & replay boundaries

One shared histogram gradient-boosted regressor, absolute-error loss, trained across 469 products. The simulator exports its numeric tree rules and evaluates them in JavaScript. It does not retrain during the replay. Stock code and product name are not model features.

Every forecast in the replay is one week ahead of recorded history. Advancing a week reveals that week's real sales and hands them to the next week's feature row; the model is never fed its own predictions, so the errors you see do not compound. A genuine four-week-out forecast would have to reuse predictions as inputs, and this model was neither trained nor validated that way.

Shapley values are computed exactly for four feature groups against the fixed 16-row reference, using the clipped-at-zero model output. They are not individual-feature TreeSHAP, causal effects, or confidence intervals. What-if adjustments may be unlike training data. Recorded actuals stay fixed and cannot validate a hypothetical change in demand.
