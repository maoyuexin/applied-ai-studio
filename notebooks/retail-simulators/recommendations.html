Model, data & explanation boundaries

Item-item collaborative filtering, cosine similarity, 15 outgoing neighbors per product. Scores are sums of similarity links from distinct purchased products, not purchase probabilities. Buying the same product twice does not raise its score. Purchase history changes here; the similarity model never retrains.

The classroom policy requires five distinct products before a customer is personalized. Cold-start and zero-signal slots use recent-revenue fallback; if that list is exhausted, remaining slots use training popularity. Only the purchase above ranks a product's nearest neighbors and makes no customer-level claim. Popular with everyone uses training-customer counts and has no personalized explanation. Ties are resolved by stock-code order.

The displayed links are exact contributions to this model's score, not reasons a person wants a product or evidence of causal sales lift. Neither offer exposure nor a discount-response experiment is available; this is Next Best Product, not offer optimization.
