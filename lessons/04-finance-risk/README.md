# 04 · AI in Finance and Risk Management

Where AI runs in a bank and why every decision has a price. Two cases: a **credit-risk review**
model that ranks accounts for an analyst, and **complaint routing** that sends a customer letter to
the right specialist team, compared with an LLM. The lesson also covers explainability, fair
lending and governance case studies.
