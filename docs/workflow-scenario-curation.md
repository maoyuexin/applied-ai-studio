# Workflow Scenario Curation

**Decision date:** 2026-08-05
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-10
**Scope:** End-to-end business workflows; executable demos remain separate.

## Depth standard

Online Order Decisions is the reference. A retained workflow must support all of
the following without adding artificial steps:

1. One coherent input-to-outcome business story.
2. At least six accountable actors or system lanes.
3. At least twelve meaningful workflow nodes.
4. At least six decisions or decision-like generation points.
5. At least three genuine exception paths or feedback loops.
6. Mixed interventions across rules, automation, optimization, AI, and humans.
7. At least two AI-relevant decisions that support Data, Method, Metric, and
   Human design.
8. A measurable synthetic operating baseline.
9. A clear human authority boundary for consequential action.
10. A plain-language introduction covering business context, workflow scope,
    process boundaries, and participants.
11. Enough accessibility and distinctiveness to justify its place.

The catalog test enforces the structural floor: six lanes, twelve nodes, fifteen
paths, six decisions, and five baseline measures. Content review remains
necessary because counts alone do not make a useful workflow.

## Promotion rule

A deferred case should return to Industry Workflows only after it has a validated
workflow-scenario reference, meets the structural floor, includes explicit
least-expensive interventions for every decision, passes desktop and 320 px
workflow checks, explains the business context for a non-domain reader, and
receives a visual collision review in both documented and actual views.
